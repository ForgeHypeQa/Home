Case study № 012026 · Online grocery · Doha · Qatar

An online grocery where 12 items take 5 minutes, not 30.

Veggify ran on a Shopify template, with separate apps behind it. Ordering was slow for shoppers and the numbers were hard to see for the team. Now the shop, the admin and the data sit in one platform that Veggify controls.

§ 01 · The brief

What Veggify needed.

Customers liked the products, but ordering took too long. The team saw the sales, but not the profit. Three things had to change.

  1. 01

    Faster ordering

    A 12-item order took about 30 minutes of searching, one item at a time.

  2. 02

    One set of numbers

    Costs, expenses and profit sat in different apps, so net profit took work to find.

  3. 03

    Less manual work

    Prices, supplier orders and minimum quantities were handled one by one.

§ 02 · What was solved

Nine problems, solved.

  • ProblemBeforeNow

  • Ordering time

    BeforeAbout 30 minutes for a 12-item order.

    NowAbout 5 minutes. Shoppers type, speak or snap their list.

  • Repeat orders

    BeforeEvery order started from zero.

    NowA personal favourites list for each customer.

  • Occasions

    BeforeParty and festive shopping rebuilt from memory.

    NowOccasion lists bring back last time’s items, plus what others bought.

  • Net profit

    BeforeSpread across several separate apps.

    NowCost, expenses and net profit in one admin.

  • Price changes

    BeforeOne product at a time.

    NowBulk price update across hundreds of products.

  • Supplier orders

    BeforeChecked and sent by hand.

    NowSent to each supplier on WhatsApp. Review takes minutes.

  • Lost shoppers

    BeforeNo view of who was browsing.

    NowA leads view, and abandoned carts get an automatic follow-up.

  • Minimums and discounts

    BeforeMinimum order and quantity discounts were hard to manage.

    NowBuilt into every product.

  • Catalogue

    BeforeDuplicates, and items never ordered once.

    NowOne clean listing per product.

§ 04 · Control

Their data. Their prices. Their call.

01

Full control of data

Veggify owns its database. Any table exports to a spreadsheet in one click. An automatic backup copies all 15 tables to their own Google Sheet, nightly, weekly or monthly. A change log shows who changed what.

02

Bulk price update

Pick the products and enter the price and cost per kilo once. Every pack size is worked out from its weight. Up to 500 products change in one action.

03

Everything connected
