Websites and apps

How much does a website cost in Qatar?

It depends on the number of pages, whether you need an online store, Arabic and English versions, and how much content we create for you. A focused business website costs far less than a full e-commerce store. After the free consultation you get a fixed quote with no surprises.

I already have a website. Can you fix it or rebuild it?

Yes. We start with an audit of your current site's speed, mobile experience, search visibility and how well it turns visits into enquiries. If the foundation is sound we fix what holds it back. If it costs more to patch than to rebuild, we tell you plainly and plan a rebuild that keeps your existing Google rankings.

Will my website be SEO friendly?

Yes. Every site we build loads fast on mobile, has one clear heading per page, unique titles and descriptions, clean URLs, a sitemap and structured data that Google and AI assistants can read. We also connect it to Google Search Console so you can see how it performs.

Will my website work in both English and Arabic?

Yes. We build bilingual websites, menus and content, with Arabic laid out right to left where it matters, and each language set up so Google can show the right version to the right visitor.

What platforms do you build websites on?

How does ForgeHype approach UI/UX and website design?

SEO and AI search

What is SEO and why does it matter for businesses in Qatar?

SEO (search engine optimization) is how your business gets found on Google without paying for every click. Most customers in Qatar search on their phone before they visit, call or message, so if you do not appear in Google Search and Google Maps, they find a competitor first.

How long does SEO take to show results in Qatar?

Fixes to your Google Business Profile and website basics can show within weeks. Ranking for competitive searches usually takes three to six months of steady work, depending on your industry, your competitors and the state of your website. We report progress every month so you can see what is moving.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings on Google?

No, and no honest consultant can, because Google decides rankings. What we do commit to is a thorough audit, a clear plan, fixes that follow Google's own guidelines and monthly reports that show your progress.

Do you handle SEO in Arabic and English?

Yes. People in Qatar search in both languages, often for the same thing. We research the keywords customers actually use in Arabic and English and check that your website and Google profile cover both.

Does ForgeHype offer AI search optimization and GEO services?

Yes. Generative engine optimization (GEO) helps your business get mentioned when people ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot or Google's AI answers for a recommendation. We audit how AI assistants and Google see your business, then give your team a clear report covering structured data, consistent business details across listings, and plain answers to the questions your customers ask.

What are LLMO and GEO?

LLMO (large language model optimization) and GEO (generative engine optimization) are about being recommended by AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot, and in Google's AI answers. It builds on SEO: clear facts about your business, consistent details across listings, structured data and content that answers real customer questions.

How do I know if ChatGPT mentions my business?

We test the questions your customers are likely to ask AI assistants, such as "best website designer in Doha", and record whether and how your business appears. We repeat the same checks each month so you can see whether your visibility in AI answers is improving.

Does ForgeHype provide performance marketing and SEO services?

We are a consultancy, not an advertising agency. We run SEO and AI visibility audits, set up analytics and lead tracking, plan your campaigns and landing pages, and review the results with you every month. Your own team or your ad partner runs the ads day to day, guided by our plan and reports.
